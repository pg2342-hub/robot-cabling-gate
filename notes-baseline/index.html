Checks

mode_correctThe note gets the right failure mode from the fixed list.
owner_correctThe note goes to the team that owns the fix.
safety_caughtcriticalEvery note about a person near the robot, an e-stop, or contact is flagged as safety.
no_false_safetyNotes with no person, e-stop or contact are not flagged as safety.
blocker_caughtcriticalA live cable disturbed, or the robot moving toward or touching a person, blocks unsupervised work.
no_false_blockerOther notes do not block unsupervised work.
review_when_unclearUnclear notes go to a person; clear notes do not.
